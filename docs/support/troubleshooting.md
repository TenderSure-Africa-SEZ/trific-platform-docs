# Troubleshooting

Common issues and their solutions for the Trific platform.

## Login Issues

### Can't sign in to your account

1. **Verify your credentials**: Double-check your email and password
2. **Clear browser cache**: Clear cookies and cached data
3. **Try incognito mode**: Test in a private browser window
4. **Reset password**: Use the forgot password feature
5. **Check account status**: Ensure your account isn't suspended

### Two-factor authentication problems

1. **Time sync**: Ensure your device time is accurate
2. **Backup codes**: Use saved backup codes if available
3. **App update**: Update your authenticator app
4. **Contact support**: For persistent issues

## Payment Issues

### Payment not processing

1. **Check payment details**: Verify card information is correct
2. **Sufficient funds**: Ensure adequate account balance
3. **Bank restrictions**: Check for international transaction blocks
4. **Try alternative method**: Use a different payment method
5. **Contact your bank**: Verify no holds on your account

### Escrow payment stuck

1. **Check milestone status**: Verify completion requirements
2. **Client approval needed**: Ensure client has reviewed work
3. **Review period**: Wait for automatic release after review period
4. **Dispute process**: Initiate dispute if needed

## Profile and Account Issues

### Profile not updating

1. **Refresh page**: Try reloading the page
2. **Clear cache**: Clear browser cache and cookies
3. **Check file formats**: Ensure uploads meet requirements
4. **Internet connection**: Verify stable internet connection

### Verification pending

1. **Document quality**: Ensure documents are clear and readable
2. **Required information**: Check all required fields are completed
3. **Processing time**: Allow 3-5 business days for review
4. **Follow up**: Contact support after waiting period

## Communication Issues

### Messages not sending

1. **Internet connection**: Check your connection stability
2. **Message length**: Ensure message meets length requirements
3. **Attachments**: Verify file size and format limits
4. **Recipient status**: Confirm recipient account is active

### Notifications not received

1. **Email settings**: Check spam/junk folders
2. **Notification preferences**: Review your notification settings
3. **Email whitelist**: Add our domain to your safe senders
4. **Contact information**: Verify your email address is current

## Performance Issues

### Platform loading slowly

1. **Internet speed**: Test your internet connection
2. **Browser update**: Ensure you're using the latest browser version
3. **Clear cache**: Remove temporary files and cookies
4. **Disable extensions**: Try disabling browser extensions
5. **Server status**: Check our status page for ongoing issues

### Mobile app problems

1. **App update**: Install the latest app version
2. **Device restart**: Restart your mobile device
3. **Storage space**: Free up device storage
4. **Reinstall app**: Uninstall and reinstall the application

## Getting Additional Help

If these solutions don't resolve your issue:

1. **Search documentation**: Browse our comprehensive guides
2. **Community forum**: Check user discussions
3. **Contact support**: Submit a detailed support ticket
4. **Live chat**: Use our live chat during business hours
5. **Video support**: Schedule a screen-sharing session
