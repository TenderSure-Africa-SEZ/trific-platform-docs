# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers about the Trific platform.

## General Questions

### What is Trific?

Trific is a comprehensive platform that connects clients with verified service providers across various industries, facilitating secure project collaboration and payments.

### How do I get started?

1. Create an account (client or provider)
2. Complete your profile
3. Browse services or post jobs
4. Start collaborating!

### Is Trific free to use?

Account creation and browsing are free. We charge transaction fees on completed projects to maintain platform quality and security.

## For Clients

### How do I find the right provider?

Use our advanced search and filtering system to find providers based on skills, location, ratings, and budget.

### How are payments protected?

All payments go through our secure escrow system, protecting your funds until project completion.

### What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

We have a comprehensive dispute resolution process and quality guarantees to ensure your satisfaction.

## For Providers

### How do I become a verified provider?

Complete our thorough vetting process including identity verification, skill assessment, and background checks.

### How do I get paid?

Payments are released from escrow upon client approval or milestone completion, typically within 24-48 hours.

### What are the platform fees?

Provider fees range from 5-15% depending on your membership level and transaction volume.

## Technical Support

### How do I reset my password?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page or contact support for assistance.

### The platform isn't working properly

Check our [troubleshooting guide](/support/troubleshooting) or contact technical support.

### How do I delete my account?

Contact support to initiate the account deletion process. Note that this action cannot be undone.
