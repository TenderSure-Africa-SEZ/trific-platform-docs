## Before Contacting Support

To help us assist you better, please:

1. **Check our documentation**: Review relevant guides and FAQ
2. **Prepare details**: Have your account information ready
3. **Describe the issue**: Provide clear, detailed descriptions
4. **Include screenshots**: Visual aids help us understand problems
5. **List steps taken**: Tell us what you've already tried

## Information to Include

When contacting support, please provide:

-   **Account email address**
-   **Problem description**
-   **Steps to reproduce the issue**
-   **Browser and device information**
-   **Screenshots or error messages**
-   **Urgency level**
