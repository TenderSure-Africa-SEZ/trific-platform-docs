## Client Satisfaction Analysis

### Rating System Understanding

**5-Star Rating Breakdown**

-   **5 Stars (Exceptional):** Exceeded expectations significantly
-   **4 Stars (Great):** Met expectations with some value-added elements
-   **3 Stars (Good):** Met basic expectations satisfactorily
-   **2 Stars (Fair):** Some issues but acceptable overall
-   **1 Star (Poor):** Did not meet expectations, significant issues

**Rating Impact Analysis**

```
Rating Distribution Impact:
- 90%+ ratings of 4-5 stars: Top tier provider status
- 80-89% ratings of 4-5 stars: Strong performance
- 70-79% ratings of 4-5 stars: Good, room for improvement
- 60-69% ratings of 4-5 stars: Needs attention
- <60% ratings of 4-5 stars: Risk of account review
```

### Client Retention Strategies

**Retention Rate Calculation**

```
Client Retention Formula:
((Clients at End of Period - New Clients) / Clients at Start) × 100

Example:
Started with: 20 clients
Ended with: 25 clients
New clients: 8 clients
Retention = ((25 - 8) / 20) × 100 = 85%
```

**Retention Improvement Tactics**

-   Regular check-ins and relationship maintenance
-   Proactive service offerings and value delivery
-   Loyalty programs and preferred provider benefits
-   Continuous service quality improvement
-   Personal relationship building and client advocacy
