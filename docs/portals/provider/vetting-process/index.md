# Provider Vetting Process

The TRIFIC vetting process ensures platform quality by thoroughly evaluating provider credentials, capabilities, and professional standards before approval.

## Vetting Philosophy

TRIFIC maintains a curated marketplace of high-quality service providers through a comprehensive vetting process that evaluates:

-   **Professional Credentials:** Verified licenses, certifications, and qualifications
-   **Business Legitimacy:** Proper business registration and compliance
-   **Service Quality:** Track record and client satisfaction history
-   **Financial Stability:** Business viability and professional insurance coverage

## Vetting Criteria & Standards

### Professional Qualifications

**Individual Providers**

-   Minimum 3 years relevant professional experience
-   Current professional license or certification in service area
-   Demonstrated expertise through work samples or case studies
-   Professional references from previous clients or employers

**Business Entities (Firms)**

-   Established business registration (minimum 2 years)
-   Professional liability insurance coverage
-   Qualified key personnel with relevant expertise
-   Financial stability and business sustainability

### Quality Assessment Framework

**Service Delivery Standards**

-   Proven track record of successful project completion
-   Client satisfaction ratings and testimonials
-   Ability to meet deadlines and quality expectations
-   Professional communication and client relationship skills

**Technical Competency**

-   Demonstrated expertise in claimed service areas
-   Up-to-date knowledge of industry best practices
-   Ability to adapt to client-specific requirements
-   Continuous professional development evidence

## Vetting Process Stages

### Stage 1: Document Verification

**Identity & Credentials Verification**

-   Government-issued ID verification
-   Professional license validation with issuing authorities
-   Educational credential confirmation
-   Employment history verification

**Business Documentation Review**

-   Certificate of incorporation/business registration
-   Tax registration and compliance status
-   Professional liability insurance verification
-   Financial statements and business viability assessment

### Stage 2: Background Checks

**Professional Background**

-   Previous employer references
-   Client testimonial verification
-   Professional association membership
-   Disciplinary action or complaint history

**Legal & Compliance Checks**

-   Criminal background screening (where legally permitted)
-   Regulatory compliance verification
-   Professional misconduct database searches
-   Litigation history review

### Stage 3: Capability Assessment

**Portfolio Review**

-   Work sample evaluation for quality and complexity
-   Case study analysis for problem-solving ability
-   Technical skill assessment through submissions
-   Innovation and creativity demonstration

**Reference Validation**

-   Contact with provided professional references
-   Verification of client testimonials and ratings
-   Confirmation of project completion and satisfaction
-   Assessment of professional relationship management

### Stage 4: Final Evaluation

**Comprehensive Review**

-   Multi-factor scoring across all vetting criteria
-   Review committee evaluation and discussion
-   Final approval or rejection decision
-   Feedback preparation for applicant communication

## Vetting Timeline & Process

| Stage                 | Duration          | Activities                          | Outcome                              |
| --------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Document Verification | 24-48 hours       | Credential and identity checks      | Documents validated or flagged       |
| Background Checks     | 2-3 business days | Professional and legal verification | Background cleared or concerns noted |
| Capability Assessment | 1-2 business days | Portfolio and reference review      | Capability confirmed or questioned   |
| Final Evaluation      | 1 business day    | Committee review and decision       | Approval or rejection with feedback  |

**Total Process Time:** 3-5 business days for complete applications

## Vetting Decision Outcomes

### Approval Categories

**Full Approval**

-   Immediate platform access granted
-   All service categories approved
-   Standard provider status with full features
-   Welcome package and onboarding support

**Conditional Approval**

-   Limited service category access
-   Probationary period with monitoring
-   Requirement to demonstrate performance
-   Re-evaluation after specified timeframe

**Provisional Approval**

-   Temporary approval pending document updates
-   Limited platform access
-   Requirement to submit missing documents
-   Full approval upon completion

### Rejection Categories

**Professional Deficiency**

-   Insufficient experience or qualifications
-   Unable to verify credentials
-   Poor reference feedback
-   Inadequate service quality demonstration

**Documentation Issues**

-   Missing or invalid required documents
-   Unable to verify business registration
-   Insurance coverage insufficient
-   Financial stability concerns

**Compliance Concerns**

-   Background check issues
-   Regulatory compliance problems
-   Previous platform violations
-   Legal or ethical concerns

## Appeals & Re-application Process

### Appeal Process

1. **Appeal Submission:** Within 30 days of rejection notification
2. **Additional Evidence:** Provide new documentation or clarifications
3. **Re-evaluation:** Independent review of appeal and new evidence
4. **Final Decision:** Communicated within 5 business days
5. **Resolution:** Approval reversal or appeal denial with detailed explanation

### Re-application Guidelines

-   Wait period: Minimum 6 months after rejection
-   Address all previously identified deficiencies
-   Provide updated documentation and references
-   Demonstrate professional development or improvement
-   Pay new vetting fee for complete re-assessment

## Quality Maintenance & Ongoing Compliance

### Periodic Re-vetting

-   Annual documentation renewal requirements
-   License and certification expiration monitoring
-   Insurance coverage verification
-   Performance evaluation and client feedback review

### Continuous Monitoring

-   Client satisfaction tracking and ratings
-   Complaint investigation and resolution
-   Performance metrics evaluation
-   Platform compliance monitoring

### Compliance Actions

**Warning System**

-   First warning: Performance coaching and support
-   Second warning: Probationary status and monitoring
-   Third warning: Account suspension pending review

**Suspension Criteria**

-   Consistent poor performance ratings
-   Client complaints about service quality
-   Documentation compliance failures
-   Platform policy violations

**Account Termination**

-   Serious professional misconduct
-   Legal or regulatory violations
-   Fraud or misrepresentation
-   Repeated platform policy violations
