# Provider Portal

The provider portal enables service providers to manage their business, find opportunities, and deliver services to clients.

## Features

### Dashboard

Overview of jobs, earnings, and performance metrics.

### Onboarding

Complete registration and verification process.

### Vetting Process

Submit credentials and complete platform vetting.

### Profile Management

Manage service offerings, portfolio, and business information.

### Job Board

Browse and apply for available jobs.

### Contract Management

Manage active contracts and deliverables.

### Messaging

Communicate with clients and support.

### Payments & Earnings

Track earnings and manage payment methods.
