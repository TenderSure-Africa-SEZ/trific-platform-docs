## Payment Processing & Methods

### Withdrawal Options

**Bank Transfer (ACH/Wire)**

```
Bank Transfer Details:
- Domestic transfers: 1-3 business days
- International transfers: 3-5 business days
- Minimum withdrawal: $50 USD equivalent
- Processing fees: $2.50 domestic, $15 international
- Maximum daily limit: $10,000 USD
```

**Mobile Money Services**

-   Regional services: M-Pesa, MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money
-   Instant to 2-hour processing times
-   Minimum withdrawal: $10 USD equivalent
-   Processing fees: 1.5-2.5% of transaction amount
-   Maximum limits vary by service and region

**Digital Wallets**

-   PayPal, Skrill, Payoneer integration
-   Processing time: 2-24 hours
-   Minimum withdrawal: $25 USD equivalent
-   Processing fees: 1-3% plus fixed fee
-   Currency conversion at competitive rates

**Cryptocurrency Options**

-   Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC supported (where legally available)
-   Near-instant processing (subject to network confirmation)
-   Minimum withdrawal: $100 USD equivalent
-   Network fees apply (varies by cryptocurrency)
-   Enhanced privacy and international accessibility

### Withdrawal Process

**Standard Withdrawal Steps**

1. **Access Wallet:** Navigate to earnings and payments section
2. **Select Method:** Choose preferred withdrawal method
3. **Enter Amount:** Specify withdrawal amount (minimum limits apply)
4. **Verify Details:** Confirm payment method information
5. **Submit Request:** Authorize withdrawal with security verification
6. **Track Status:** Monitor processing status and receive confirmations

**Required Information by Method**

```
Bank Transfer Requirements:
- Full name (as registered with bank)
- Bank account number
- Routing/SWIFT code
- Bank address and branch information

Mobile Money Requirements:
- Registered phone number
- Mobile money provider
- Account holder name verification

Digital Wallet Requirements:
- Wallet email address
- Account verification status
- Preferred currency for receipt
```

## Tax Obligations & Compliance

### Tax Requirements by Jurisdiction

**United States Providers**

-   Form 1099-NEC for earnings over $600 annually
-   Quarterly estimated tax payments for self-employment
-   Business expense deductions and record keeping
-   State tax obligations and registration requirements
-   Sales tax considerations for applicable services

**International Providers**

```
Common Tax Considerations:
- VAT registration and remittance (EU providers)
- GST obligations (Australia, Canada, New Zealand)
- Income tax reporting in country of residence
- Double taxation treaty benefits and applications
- Withholding tax on US-sourced income
```

**Tax Withholding**

-   Automatic withholding for certain jurisdictions
