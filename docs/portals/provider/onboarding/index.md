# Provider Onboarding Guide

The TRIFIC provider onboarding process ensures that all service providers meet platform standards and are properly verified before serving clients.

## Onboarding Overview

The provider onboarding consists of four key phases designed to verify credentials, assess capabilities, and integrate new providers into the TRIFIC ecosystem.

### Phase 1: Registration & Basic Information

**Account Setup**

-   Create provider account with business email
-   Select provider type (Individual, Firm, Enterprise)
-   Complete basic business information form
-   Verify email address and set secure password

**Initial Requirements**

-   Business name and registration details
-   Primary contact information
-   Service category selection
-   Geographic service areas

### Phase 2: Business Profile & Questionnaire

**Comprehensive Business Assessment**

-   Detailed questionnaire about business background
-   Years of experience and team composition
-   Professional qualifications and certifications
-   Previous client work and references

**Service Definition**

-   Primary service offerings description
-   Specialized expertise areas
-   Typical project sizes and duration
-   Preferred client engagement models

### Phase 3: Document Submission & Verification

**Required Documentation (KYV - Know Your Vendor)**

**Individual Providers:**

-   Government-issued photo ID
-   Professional license/certification
-   Business registration certificate
-   Tax identification documents
-   Professional liability insurance

**Firm/Enterprise Providers:**

-   Certificate of incorporation
-   Business operating licenses
-   Tax registration documents
-   Professional liability insurance
-   Key personnel qualifications
-   Financial statements (last 2 years)

**Document Requirements**

-   High-quality scans or photos
-   All documents must be current and valid
-   English translation required for foreign documents
-   Maximum file size: 25MB per document

### Phase 4: Vetting Fee & Final Review

**Vetting Fee Structure**

-   Individual Providers: $99 USD
-   Small Firms (2-10 employees): $199 USD
-   Medium Firms (11-50 employees): $399 USD
-   Large Firms (51+ employees): $799 USD

**Payment Options**

-   Credit/debit card processing
-   Bank transfer
-   Mobile money services (region-specific)
-   Cryptocurrency (where available)

**Review Process**

-   TRIFIC verification team reviews all submissions
-   Background checks and credential verification
-   Reference checks with previous clients
-   Assessment of service quality standards
-   Decision communicated within 3-5 business days

## Common Issues & Solutions

**Document Upload Problems**

-   Ensure file size is under 25MB
-   Use PDF, JPG, or PNG formats only
-   Verify document clarity and completeness
-   Check internet connection stability

**Payment Processing Issues**

-   Verify card details and expiration date
-   Ensure sufficient funds or credit limit
-   Try alternative payment methods
-   Contact payment support for assistance

**Review Delays**

-   Typical review takes 3-5 business days
-   Holiday periods may extend timelines
-   Incomplete documents cause delays
-   Check email for additional requirements

## Approval & Next Steps

**Upon Approval**

-   Welcome email with platform access instructions
-   Profile setup guidance and best practices
-   Initial portfolio creation recommendations
-   First job application strategies

**Platform Access**

-   Full provider portal functionality
-   Job board access and application capabilities
-   Client communication tools
-   Earnings and payment management

**Getting Started Recommendations**

1. Complete professional portfolio (aim for 100% completion)
2. Set competitive but realistic rate cards
3. Upload high-quality portfolio samples
4. Apply to first job opportunities
5. Engage with platform community features

## Quality Standards & Expectations

**Professional Standards**

-   Maintain high-quality service delivery
-   Respond to client communications promptly
-   Meet project deadlines consistently
-   Provide accurate project estimates

**Platform Compliance**

-   Follow TRIFIC terms of service
-   Maintain current documentation and licenses
-   Report any changes to business status
-   Participate in periodic compliance reviews
