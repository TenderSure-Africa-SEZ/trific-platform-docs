# Management Portal

The management portal provides business stakeholders with strategic oversight and operational control of the platform.

## Features

### Executive Dashboard

High-level business metrics and KPIs.

### Financial Management

Revenue tracking, cost analysis, and financial reporting.

### Strategic Analytics

Business intelligence and market insights.

### Operations Management

Operational oversight and process optimization.

### Partner Management

Manage strategic partnerships and integrations.
