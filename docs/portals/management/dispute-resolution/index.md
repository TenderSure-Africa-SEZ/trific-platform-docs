**Management-Level Case Categories**

-   Executive escalation disputes requiring senior leadership involvement
-   High-financial-impact cases exceeding standard resolution thresholds
-   Complex multi-party disputes involving multiple stakeholders
-   Legal or regulatory compliance disputes requiring expert analysis

**Priority Assessment Framework**

```
Dispute Priority Classification:
Critical Priority (Immediate Response):
- Legal threats or regulatory compliance violations
- High-value financial disputes (>$50,000)
- Strategic client or key provider disputes
- Platform reputation or brand impact cases

High Priority (Response within 24 hours):
- Financial disputes requiring management approval
- Complex service quality or delivery disputes
- Provider certification or compliance violations
- Client satisfaction impact with expansion implications

Medium Priority (Response within 72 hours):
- Standard escalation from operational dispute resolution
- Policy interpretation disputes requiring clarification
- Multi-party coordination and mediation cases
- Process improvement opportunity identification

Low Priority (Response within 1 week):
- Documentation and precedent establishment
- Process optimization and efficiency improvement
- Training and development opportunity identification
- System enhancement and feature development insights
```

## Resolution Decision Framework

### Decision-Making Process

**Systematic Resolution Methodology**

-   Comprehensive evidence review and stakeholder position analysis
-   Platform policy and legal compliance verification
-   Precedent review and consistency evaluation with previous decisions
-   Financial impact assessment and business relationship consideration

**Resolution Decision Categories**

```
Resolution Decision Framework:
Financial Resolutions:
├── Full Client Refund
│   ├── Complete project failure or non-delivery
│   ├── Material provider breach of contract
│   ├── Platform service failure causing client loss
│   └── Quality standards not met with no remediation possible
├── Partial Refund/Release
│   ├── Partial project delivery with acceptable quality
│   ├── Delivery delays with compensation adjustment
│   ├── Scope changes requiring financial adjustment
│   └── Mutual agreement on value delivered assessment
├── Escrow Release to Provider
│   ├── Project completion meeting quality standards
│   ├── Client acceptance despite minor concerns
│   ├── Contract fulfillment with acceptable delivery
│   └── Dispute resolution favoring provider position
└── Alternative Resolution
    ├── Mediated settlement with negotiated terms
    ├── Service credit or platform benefit compensation
    ├── Additional work or remediation requirements
    └── Account restriction or monitoring implementation
```
