# Pricing

Transparent pricing for the Trific platform services.

## Pricing Overview

Our pricing structure is designed to be transparent, competitive, and aligned with the value we deliver to both clients and providers.

## For Clients

### Transaction Fees

-   **Standard Projects**: 3% of project value
-   **Enterprise Clients**: Custom pricing available
-   **Minimum Fee**: $5 per transaction
-   **No Monthly Fees**: Pay only when you use the platform

### Additional Services

-   **Priority Support**: $29/month
-   **Advanced Analytics**: $49/month
-   **Custom Integration**: Starting at $500

### Payment Processing

-   **Credit Cards**: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
-   **Bank Transfers**: $0.50 per transaction
-   **International**: Additional 1% for currency conversion

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Pricing

**Q: Are there any hidden fees?**
A: No. All fees are clearly disclosed upfront with no hidden charges.

**Q: Can I change my plan anytime?**
A: Yes. Plan changes take effect immediately with prorated billing.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**
A: Transaction fees are non-refundable, but subscription fees can be refunded within 30 days.

### Transaction Fees

**Q: When are transaction fees charged?**
A: Fees are deducted when payments are released from escrow.

**Q: Are fees negotiable for large projects?**
A: Enterprise clients can discuss custom pricing for significant volumes.

### International Pricing

**Q: Are there additional fees for international transactions?**
A: Yes, a 1% currency conversion fee applies to international transactions.

**Q: Which currencies do you support?**
A: USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, and 15+ additional currencies.
