# Payment & Wallet Management

The integrated payment system provides secure financial management for all client transactions, including wallet funding, escrow management, and payment processing.

## Overview

The payment system handles all financial aspects of the client experience, from initial wallet funding through project payments and final settlements, ensuring security and transparency throughout the process.

### Core Capabilities

-   **Wallet Management:** Centralized fund storage and balance tracking
-   **Escrow Services:** Secure holding of project funds until milestone completion
-   **Payment Processing:** Multiple payment methods with secure gateway integration
-   **Transaction History:** Complete record of all financial activities
-   **Automated Payments:** Milestone-based automatic payment releases

## Wallet System

### Wallet Features

-   **Balance Display:** Real-time available funds and committed escrow amounts
-   **Transaction Tracking:** Detailed history of all deposits, payments, and refunds
-   **Multi-Currency Support:** Handle different currencies based on project requirements
-   **Security Measures:** Bank-level encryption and fraud protection
-   **Account Integration:** Seamless connection with external payment methods

### Funding Methods

#### PayPal Integration

-   **Instant Funding:** Immediate availability of PayPal balance transfers
-   **Linked Accounts:** Support for bank accounts and cards linked to PayPal
-   **Transaction Limits:** Up to $10,000 per transaction, $50,000 monthly
-   **Processing Time:** Instant for PayPal balance, 1-3 days for bank transfers
-   **Fees:** 2.9% + $0.30 for credit card funding, free for bank transfers

#### Bank Transfer

-   **Direct Deposit:** ACH transfers from verified bank accounts
-   **Processing Time:** 1-3 business days for standard transfers
-   **Same-Day Options:** Express transfers available for additional fee
-   **Security:** Bank-grade encryption and verification processes
-   **Fees:** Free for standard transfers, $15 for same-day processing

#### M-Pesa Integration

-   **Mobile Money:** Direct mobile money transfers in supported regions
-   **Instant Processing:** Immediate fund availability
-   **Regional Availability:** Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, and expanding markets
-   **Transaction Limits:** Based on M-Pesa account tier and verification level
-   **Fees:** Standard M-Pesa rates apply plus 1% platform fee

#### Credit/Debit Cards

-   **Immediate Funding:** Instant availability for escrow funding
-   **Card Types:** Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover
-   **Security:** PCI DSS compliant processing with tokenization
-   **Fees:** 3.5% + $0.30 per transaction
-   **Limits:** $5,000 per transaction, $25,000 monthly

## Escrow Management

### Escrow Fundamentals

The escrow system protects both clients and providers by holding project funds securely until work is completed and approved.

#### How Escrow Works

1. **Funding:** Client deposits milestone payments into secure escrow account
2. **Protection:** Funds held safely while provider completes work
3. **Release:** Automatic payment to provider upon client milestone approval
4. **Security:** Funds cannot be accessed by either party during work phase
5. **Dispute Protection:** Escrow handles fund distribution in case of disputes

### Milestone-Based Payments

-   **Phased Funding:** Fund one or more milestones at a time
-   **Flexible Timing:** Add funds as project progresses or fund entire project upfront
-   **Progress Tracking:** Visual indicators showing funded vs. unfunded milestones
-   **Automatic Releases:** Payments triggered by client milestone approvals
-   **Change Management:** Adjust escrow amounts when project scope changes

### Escrow Status Tracking

-   **Available Balance:** Funds in wallet available for new escrow commitments
-   **Committed Funds:** Money allocated to active project milestones
-   **Pending Releases:** Approved milestones with payments processing
-   **Released Payments:** Completed payments transferred to providers
-   **Refunded Amounts:** Funds returned from cancelled or disputed projects

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Q: Why is my payment method being declined?**
A: Check with your bank or card issuer about transaction limits or fraud holds. Verify billing information matches your account.

**Q: How long do refunds take to process?**
A: Refunds typically take 3-5 business days to appear in your original payment method, though some banks may take longer.

**Q: Can I change my payment method for ongoing projects?**
A: You cannot change the source of already-escrowed funds, but you can use different methods for future milestone funding.

**Q: What happens if there's a dispute over payment?**
A: The dispute resolution process will review all evidence and make a binding decision on fund distribution.
