# Client Portal

The client portal provides tools and interfaces for clients to manage their projects and interactions with service providers.

## Features

### Dashboard

Overview of projects, contracts, and activities.

### Provider Discovery

Find and evaluate service providers.

### Contract Management

Create, manage, and track service contracts.

### Job Management

Post jobs, track progress, and manage deliverables.

### Messaging

Communicate with providers and support.

### Payments

Handle payments, invoicing, and financial transactions.

### Reviews & Ratings

Rate providers and leave feedback.

### User Management

Manage account settings and preferences.
