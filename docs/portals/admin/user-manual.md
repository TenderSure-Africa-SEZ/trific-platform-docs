## Provider Vetting

### Vetting Queue Management

**Application Review Process**

1. **Queue Prioritization**

    - Rush applications (premium vetting fee paid)
    - Standard applications (first-in, first-out)
    - Re-submissions (previously rejected applications)
    - Flagged applications (requiring special attention)

2. **Document Verification**
    ```
    Required Documentation Checklist:
    □ Government-issued identification
    □ Professional licenses and certifications
    □ Business registration documents
    □ Financial stability proof
    □ Professional liability insurance
    □ Client references and testimonials
    ```

**Scoring Methodology**

```
Vetting Criteria and Weights:
- Professional Experience (30%): Years in practice, complexity of work
- Credentials & Certifications (25%): Relevant qualifications and licenses
- Financial Stability (20%): Business viability and insurance coverage
- References & Testimonials (15%): Client feedback and professional reputation
- Documentation Quality (10%): Completeness and authenticity of submissions
```

### Application Review Workflow

**Step 1: Initial Assessment**

-   Review application completeness
-   Verify basic eligibility requirements
-   Check for any obvious disqualifying factors
-   Flag applications requiring special attention

**Step 2: Document Verification**

-   Authenticate submitted documents
-   Verify licenses with issuing authorities
-   Confirm business registration status
-   Validate insurance coverage and limits

**Step 3: Scoring Application**

```
Scoring Process:
1. Apply standardized criteria to each category
2. Calculate weighted scores across all criteria
3. Compare against minimum threshold requirements
4. Document scoring rationale and decision factors
5. Prepare recommendation for final approval
```

**Step 4: Decision Process**

-   **Approve:** Score meets all thresholds and requirements
-   **Reject:** Score below minimum or disqualifying factors present
-   **Request Information:** Missing documents or clarification needed
-   **Escalate:** Complex cases requiring senior review

### Approval & Rejection Procedures

**Provider Approval Process**

1. **Final Review:** Confirm all requirements met and properly documented
2. **Approval Action:** Click "Approve Provider" with scoring justification
3. **Directory Update:** Provider automatically added to client directory
4. **Notification:** Welcome email sent with onboarding instructions
5. **Documentation:** Approval recorded in audit logs with decision rationale

**Provider Rejection Process**

1. **Rejection Decision:** Document specific reasons for rejection
2. **Feedback Preparation:** Provide detailed, constructive feedback
3. **Rejection Notice:** Send formal rejection with improvement guidance
4. **Appeal Information:** Include appeal process and timeline information
5. **Record Keeping:** Maintain rejection records for compliance and audit

## Compliance Management

### Compliance Monitoring

**Automated Monitoring Systems**

-   Provider performance tracking against quality standards
-   Client satisfaction monitoring and alert thresholds
-   Financial compliance and payment processing oversight
-   Regulatory requirement adherence verification

**Manual Review Triggers**

```
Compliance Review Indicators:
- Client complaints above threshold (3+ in 30 days)
- Missed deadlines or project failures (>2 consecutive)
- Payment disputes or processing issues
- License expiration or credential updates
- Regulatory changes affecting provider eligibility
```

### Enforcement Actions

**Warning System**

1. **First Warning:** Performance coaching and improvement plan
2. **Second Warning:** Probationary status with enhanced monitoring
3. **Final Warning:** Suspension threat with specific improvement requirements

**Suspension Procedures**

-   Temporary removal from directory and new project eligibility
-   Existing contracts allowed to complete under monitoring
-   Required remediation actions and timeline for reinstatement
-   Regular review of suspension status and progress

**Blacklist Process**

-   Permanent removal from platform for serious violations
-   Immediate contract termination with client protection measures
-   No eligibility for future applications or appeal after 2 years
-   Documentation of violation and evidence supporting decision

### Re-evaluation Workflows

**Scheduled Re-evaluations**

-   Annual license and certification verification
-   Performance review based on client feedback and metrics
-   Financial stability assessment and insurance verification
-   Continuing education and professional development confirmation

**Triggered Re-evaluations**

-   Significant client complaints or performance issues
-   Changes in business structure or ownership
-   Regulatory violations or legal issues
-   Market conditions affecting service delivery

### Troubleshooting Guide

**Common Administrative Issues**

**Q: Vetting queue is not loading or displaying incorrect information**
A: Check system status dashboard, clear browser cache, or contact technical support for backend issues.

**Q: Unable to approve or reject provider applications**
A: Verify scoring has been completed and meets minimum requirements. Check user permissions and system connectivity.

**Q: Compliance alerts are not generating automatically**
A: Review system settings for monitoring thresholds and notification preferences. Verify automated job scheduling.

**Q: Reports are failing to generate or export**
A: Check data permissions, report parameters, and system resources. Try generating smaller date ranges or fewer metrics.
