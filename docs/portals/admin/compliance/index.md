# Compliance Management

Comprehensive compliance management system for TRIFIC platform administrators to ensure regulatory adherence, policy enforcement, risk mitigation, and legal compliance across all platform operations and user interactions.

## Regulatory Compliance Framework

The compliance management system maintains adherence to all applicable regulations, industry standards, and legal requirements while ensuring platform integrity and user protection.

### Regulatory Environment Management

**Regulatory Landscape Monitoring**

-   Continuous monitoring of relevant regulations and legal changes
-   Impact assessment of new regulations on platform operations
-   Compliance requirement mapping and implementation planning
-   Regulatory communication and stakeholder engagement

**Compliance Framework Development**

```
Regulatory Compliance Areas:

- Employment Law: Contractor classification, labor standards

**Risk Management Matrix**

```
Compliance Risk Categories:

- Data breaches affecting personal information
