# TRIFIC Platform

## Welcome to the Future of Service Procurement

TRIFIC is a revolutionary curated marketplace that transforms how corporate clients connect with pre-vetted service providers. Say goodbye to endless bidding processes and hello to direct, quality-assured engagements.

---

## 🚀 Why Choose TRIFIC?

### **Pre-Vetted Excellence**
Every service provider on our platform undergoes rigorous TenderSure evaluation with quarterly re-assessments, ensuring you work with only the best.

### **No Bidding Required**
Skip the lengthy bidding process. Browse our curated directory and engage directly with qualified providers who meet your specific needs.

### **Secure Transactions**
Built-in escrow system with milestone-based payments and multi-gateway support protects both clients and providers throughout the engagement.

### **Complete Transparency**
Track every aspect of your projects with our comprehensive portal system designed for clients, providers, and administrators.

## 🔧 Technology Stack

Built on enterprise-grade technology for reliability and scale:

- **Backend**: Django 4.2 + Django REST Framework
- **Frontend**: Vue.js 3 + TypeScript for modern, responsive interfaces
- **Database**: PostgreSQL 15 for robust data management
- **Caching**: Redis 7 for optimal performance
- **Real-time**: Django Channels for instant messaging
- **Storage**: AWS S3 for secure file management
- **Deployment**: Docker + Kubernetes for scalable infrastructure
